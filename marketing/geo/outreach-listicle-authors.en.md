# Outreach to the authors of the 6 existing Dubai listicles — template

⛔ Draft only. Adrien sends, by hand, from his own address. Nothing has been sent.

## The angle (why they would answer at all)

Every one of these listicles is written by a competitor that ranks itself first. Asking them to
promote us is a weak ask. What we can honestly offer:

1. **We already listed them** in our own comparison, with their published figures and their real
   strengths, sourced and dated — and we invite corrections. That is a fact, not a favour traded.
2. **A complete, checkable entry** for their page: a published fixed price and timeline, which
   their readers (and the AI engines that quote them) are looking for and most entries lack.

No payment, no link exchange, no "guest post" pitch: none of the six publishes a contributor or
editorial contact, so this goes to the general/sales address, and it must read in five seconds.

Send only once the EN listicle is live (the template links to it).

## What not to do

- Do not quote "an MVP under 3 weeks cuts corners" as TekRevol's — it is not on any TekRevol page
  (the wording is SpeedMVPs'). Quoting it wrongly would sink the email.
- No follow-up chain: one email, one optional reminder after 10 days, then stop.
- No offer of payment or reciprocal links: a paid placement in a self-ranked listicle is exactly
  the signal that makes engines discount it.
