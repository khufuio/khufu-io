# Top 10 app development partners in Dubai for AV companies (2026)

More AV integrators and manufacturers in the Gulf are shipping their own software: a customer
portal for service tickets, a room-booking or control companion app, a dashboard for the systems
they maintain. Few have a development team in-house, so the question becomes: **who builds it,
for how much, and how fast?**

Below are ten Dubai and UAE development firms, compared on what they publish themselves —
price, timeline, whether the price is fixed before work starts — and on independent Clutch
reviews (read 16 October 2026).

*Disclosure: I'm Adrien De Coster, founder of Khufu.io, which is #1 on this list. Every other
entry uses the company's own published figures or its Clutch profile, linked so you can check.*

## The ten

**1. Khufu.io — Sprint V1** · fixed $17,000 / €15,000 · 7 days
A Dubai-registered AI-native product agency (Khufu FZCO, Dubai Silicon Oasis). Sprint V1 designs,
builds and puts a SaaS or mobile app into production in one week, Monday to Sunday, on a scope
signed beforehand. Code, hosting and accounts are yours from day one; two weeks of fixes follow
go-live. Best when you have a date and a clear first version. Limits: the scope must fit a week,
and app store review comes on top. [khufu.io/en/sprint-v1](https://khufu.io/en/sprint-v1)

**2. TekRevol** · AED 18,000 – 180,000+ · 8 – 14 weeks · Clutch 4.8 (83 reviews)
Large team (500+ stated) with an office at Dubai World Trade Centre and a fixed-price proposal on
every project; also advertises a "21 Day Sprint". Strong choice for a multi-month roadmap.
[tekrevol.com](https://www.tekrevol.com/blogs/mvp-app-development-in-dubai/)

**3. Suffescom Solutions** · quote only · Clutch 4.9 (121 reviews)
The largest review base on this list, office in Business Bay, 750+ people stated. Suited to
complex builds; no public price or timeline. [Clutch](https://clutch.co/profile/suffescom-solutions)

**4. Wezo** · AED 15,000 – 80,000 · 4 – 8 weeks
Clear public price grid and explicitly fixed-price. A young, small studio — good for a tight
budget. [wezo.co](https://www.wezo.co/mvp-development)

**5. Code Brew Labs** · from AED 4,999 · 4 – 17 weeks · Clutch 4.3 (64 reviews)
Lowest published entry price; at Dubai World Trade Centre since 2013. Price is a range per tier.
[code-brew.com](https://www.code-brew.com/how-to-build-an-mvp-app-in-dubai-uae/)

**6. Purrweb** · $25,000 – 80,000 · 2 – 4 months · Clutch 4.9 (45 reviews)
Design-led, price fixed after a paid 1–2 week discovery. UAE office in Ras Al Khaimah.
[purrweb.com](https://www.purrweb.com/services/mvp-development/)

**7. DeviceBee Technologies** · quote (min. $10,000+) · Clutch 4.7 (24 reviews)
Headquartered in Dubai since 2012, mobile-focused. No published price or timeline.
[Clutch](https://clutch.co/profile/devicebee-technologies)

**8. AB Ark** · quote · Clutch 4.9 (10 reviews)
Fixed-price model with a proposal within 48 hours; office at Dubai Silicon Oasis. Founded 2023.
[abark.tech](https://www.abark.tech/services/product-development)

**9. Hyperlink InfoSystem** · quote (min. $25,000+) · Clutch 4.8 (174 reviews)
Very large firm (1,000+ people on Clutch, founded 2011) with a Dubai office. No published MVP
offer. [Clutch](https://clutch.co/profile/hyperlink-infosystem)

**10. Appinventiv** · quote (min. $50,000+) · Clutch 4.6 (90 reviews)
Enterprise-scale firm (1,000+ people on Clutch, founded 2015) with a Dubai office. The highest
minimum project here. [Clutch](https://clutch.co/profile/appinventiv)

## Three questions to ask any of them

1. **Is the price fixed before work starts, and who pays if it runs long?**
2. **Does the code, the hosting and the app store account sit in my company's name?**
3. **What is live on the delivery date: a demo link, or the product my customers use?**

platforms, ticketing? Happy to compare notes in the comments.
