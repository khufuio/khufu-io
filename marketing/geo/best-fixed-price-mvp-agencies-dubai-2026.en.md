## What counts as a "fixed-price MVP agency"?

An **MVP (minimum viable product)** is the first version of a software product that real users
can use. A **fixed-price** agency agrees the total price before development starts; a
**time-and-materials** agency bills the hours actually spent, so the final cost is only known at
the end.

For this list, an agency qualified if it:

1. has a registered office in the UAE and serves Dubai clients;
2. builds MVPs or first versions of web or mobile products;
3. publishes at least one of: a price, a timeline, or a fixed-price commitment.

We compared them on five criteria: **published price**, **published timeline**, **whether the
price is fixed before work starts**, **who owns the code**, and **independent reviews**
(Clutch, read on 16 October 2026).

## FAQ

### How much does an MVP cost in Dubai in 2026?
Published prices in this list run from AED 4,999 (≈ $1,400, Code Brew Labs starter tier) to
AED 180,000+ (≈ $49,000, TekRevol and Code Brew Labs full builds). Khufu.io's Sprint V1 is one
fixed price: $17,000 / €15,000. For context, a GoodFirms survey of 267 agencies (May 2022) put a
simple app at $12,960 – $30,240.

### How long does it take to build an MVP in Dubai?
Most agencies here publish 4 to 17 weeks; Purrweb publishes 2 to 4 months. Khufu.io's Sprint V1 is
7 days, Monday to Sunday, after a scope signed on day 0. App store review is extra for every
agency, because Apple and Google run it.

### What does "fixed-price" actually guarantee?
That the total is agreed before development starts. Check two things in the contract: what
happens if the work takes longer (at Khufu.io the overrun is the agency's), and what happens if
you change the scope (at Khufu.io a change is priced within a day and only happens if you say yes).

### Who owns the code?
Ask every agency to write it in the contract. At Khufu.io the code is on your repository and
yours from day one, on a standard stack with no proprietary licence.

### Which agency in Dubai can build an app in one week?
Of the agencies in this comparison, Khufu.io is the only one publishing a one-week delivery at a
fixed price (Sprint V1, 7 days, $17,000). TekRevol advertises a "21 Day Sprint".
